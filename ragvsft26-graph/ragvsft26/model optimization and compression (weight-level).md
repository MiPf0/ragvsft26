- quantization
	- Q4_K_M
		- In the world of Large Language Models (LLMs), **Q4_K_M** is a specific "flavor" of quantization used to shrink models so they can run on consumer hardware (like your laptop) without losing too much "intelligence."
		  Think of quantization like compressing a high-definition video: you want a smaller file size, but you don't want the picture to become a blurry mess.
